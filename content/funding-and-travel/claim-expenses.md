This page explains how to claim expenses from School of Economics funding (see [funding available](/funding-and-travel/funding-available/)). Use the [ER1 form](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/finance-hub/Shared%20Documents/Forms/AllItems.aspx?id=%2fsites%2ffinance-hub%2fShared+Documents%2fstudent_expense_reimbursement_form_er1.pdf&parent=%2fsites%2ffinance-hub%2fShared+Documents&isSPOFile=1&OR=Teams-HL&CT=1727408109525) (UNSW login required) for all claims, such as Airbnb stays and conference registration fees.

1. Enter the relevant items on the ER1 form.
2. Send the completed ER1 form to Sussan Su (<q.su@unsw.edu.au>). Attach:
   - the tax invoice or receipt for each item
   - the bank or credit card transactions showing your payments
   - the relevant approval emails from Hongyi Li, the Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC).
3. Sussan gets Hongyi's signature on the form and sends the claim to Finance for processing.

## Details for the ER1 form

Enter these details on the form:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Fund | OP001 |
| Department | FBS121 |
| Project Code | PS74434 |
| Project Name | SDDRESE Res Stdnt Sppt – ECON |
