***Note: This is a sample email sent to PGRC every February and January to get conference funding approval.***

**Subject:** Conference Support Pre-Approval, Your Name, zID

**Main body:**

## Names of conferences you are applying (including the ones you do not require funding from school)

### Conference 1

- Name:
- Date:
- Place:
- Notification date for acceptance/rejection
- Whether you have discussed this with your supervisor
- Why you chose this conference: (Example: The current PhD paper I am working on is an applied public finance paper. The conference is generally quite an applied conference, with many policymakers in attendance. Another consideration is that some of the other Australian 'macro' conferences aren't being held this year (e.g. ESAM).)
- Expected Cost
  - Airfare:
  - Accommodation:
  - Registration Fee:
- Other funding sources (scientia, PRSS etc) & how much are you willing to personally contribute for this

### Conference 2

- Name:
- Date:
- Place:
- Notification date for acceptance/rejection
- Whether you have discussed this with your supervisor
- Why you chose this conference: (Example: Provides opportunity to learn political science content that may be useful for my research going forward. Many faculty members are involved in the workshop that I would like to meet and network with, e.g., AAA, BBB, CCC)
- Expected Cost
  - Airfare:
  - Accommodation:
  - Registration Fee:
- Other funding sources (scientia, PRSS etc) & how much are you willing to personally contribute for this
