## PhD Year 1

- ECON5000 Research Economics Full-Time (ECON5001 Research Economics Part-Time if you study part-time)
  - Enrol yourself via the [myUNSW website](https://my.unsw.edu.au).
  - You **must be enrolled** in this course every term, including Summer Term, for the duration of your PhD studies.
- Term 1
  - ECON7001 Microeconomic Theory I
  - ECON7004 Mathematical Economics
- Term 2
  - ECON7002 Macroeconomic Theory I
  - COMM8102 Econometric Analysis
- Term 3
  - ECON7101 Microeconomic Theory II
  - ECON7102 Macroeconomic Theory II

### Notes

- The **four courses** listed in Terms 1 and 2 are the **compulsory** core courses. You must take them, unless exceptional circumstances apply.
- We recommend you also take the second course in both the macro and microeconomics sequences offered in Term 3.
- You may also want to take **additional elective courses** during your first year.
  - Please talk with your supervisors as early as possible to determine your course load for your program.
  - It is possible you would benefit from taking courses outside the School of Economics (SOE). See below for more information about this.
- Apart from ECON5000, which you enrol in yourself, enrolment in your courses is handled by the SOE.
  - Once you have discussed with your supervisors, contact the SOE office and/or the Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC).
- ***An average grade of at least 85 in the four core courses, with a minimum of 75 marks in each, is required to continue in the PhD program. You also need an average of at least 75 in any other courses.***

## PhD Years 2–4

- ECON5000 Research Economics Full-Time (or ECON5001 if part-time)
  - Enrol yourself via the [myUNSW website](https://my.unsw.edu.au).
  - You **must be enrolled** in this course every term, including Summer Term, for the duration of your PhD studies.

There is no recommended course load for Direct Entry PhD students. Which courses you take, and when, is something you **must discuss** with your **supervisors** and the PGRC. In general, you should take courses during the first two years of your program, and leave the last two years for research. In addition to the courses offered by the SOE, you may benefit from courses from other disciplines (Finance, Mathematics and Statistics, Computer Science).

Course offerings for the current year can be found in the [UNSW Class Timetable](https://timetable.unsw.edu.au/current/ECONKENS.html).

- Economics electives offered in 2026
  - ECON7202 Empirical Methods for Causal Inference (Term 1)
  - ECON7204 Macroeconometrics (Term 2)
  - ECON7205 Microeconometrics (Term 2)
  - ECON7301 Industrial Organisation (Term 3)
  - ECON7309 Economic Measurement (Term 3)
- Check the current timetable for other years.

### Notes

- If you want to take a course that is not on the list, talk to your supervisors first, and then contact the PGRC.
- Enrolment in your coursework is handled by the SOE.
  - Once you have discussed with your supervisors, contact the SOE office and/or the PGRC.
  - If you are taking courses outside the SOE, you need to obtain permission from the course's Lecturer in Charge (LIC).
- ***An average grade of at least 75 is required to continue in the PhD program.***
