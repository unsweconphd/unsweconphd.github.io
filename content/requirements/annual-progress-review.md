## Timing of the Annual Progress Review (APR)

- You have to go through an APR within **twelve months** from the previous review (either confirmation review or a previous APR).
- Unlike the confirmation review, you are required to **present** at one of the internal seminar series.
- It is possible (even desirable) that you organise your APR prior to the twelve month period.
  - For example, you may want to give a talk that coincides with the visit of a prominent academic to receive their feedback.

## Procedure for the APR

- Talk to your **supervisors** and the PGRC about the **best timing** for your APR.
  - Do this as soon as is practical, but at least **two months** prior to the scheduled date for your APR.
  - Remember, you have to go through an APR during the twelve months after your last annual review.
  - Pick **tentative dates** that both your supervisors and panel members can attend.
- Once you have tentative dates, contact the SOE **seminar coordinator** to secure a slot for your presentation.
  - The seminar schedule is usually quite busy, so it is really important that you **plan ahead**.
- In case your supervisors find beneficial to change panel members (e.g., new staff member, change in the research topic etc.), talk to the academics that you want to have on your panel, check their availability, and more importantly seek feedback on your work.
  - Any change in the panel composition must be approved by the PGRC.
- Once the date is fixed, notify the PGRC.
  - Please notify the PGRC **at least one month in advance** so that the SOE office have enough time to organise things.
  - Specify date/time for your review.
  - Include title, abstract and, if available, a draft of the paper you want to present.
- You must fill in all the **paperwork** on [GRIS](https://gris.unsw.edu.au/login) and send relevant documents to the supervisor and panel members.
  - Fill in all the required forms on GRIS minimum **one month** prior to the review.
- It is a **requirement** of the Business School that you submit the [PhD PLO assessment](https://unsw-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/z3485733_ad_unsw_edu_au/EZdCJAJfIYBCrnbtah0T6gQB1it2PHIJc9ZPkman-gaU5w?e=rxHeNd) [form](https://unsw-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/z3485733_ad_unsw_edu_au/EZdCJAJfIYBCrnbtah0T6gQB1it2PHIJc9ZPkman-gaU5w?e=rxHeNd) signed by your supervisor and upload to GRIS.
  - Alternatively, please upload the [signed form here](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=pM_2PxXn20i44Qhnufn7o7iTWtY-FCxHt53dD3ImtK9UNjZOSkw3SFlTNEdRMDJPRjRDU1E2MU5UVSQlQCN0PWcu).
- Send the draft of the paper you are presenting to all panel members **minimum two weeks** prior to the review.
  - Ideally, you would include the paper and other relevant information when you fill in the GRIS form.
- Send the presentation material to your supervisors and panel members **at least two day** before the presentation.

## Format of the Review

- You will have **45-50 minutes** to present your own work.
  - This is slightly less than the typical academic talk in Australia (60 minutes).
- The panel members will have **10 minutes** to ask questions about your presentation and your research.
  - They will also request an update on the progress you are making towards your degree.
- You then will be asked to leave the room, while the panel discusses your progress with your supervisors.
- Once a decision has been reached, you'll be asked to come back and receive feedback from the panel.
  - The panel members will set **milestones** for the next APR.

## Minimum Requirements for the APR

- **Coursework**
  - Passing all courses with grade 75 or above.
- **PhD Seminar Series**
  - One lecture and one presentation.
  - Three referee-style reports.
- **Research Progress**
  - You must have accomplished all the **milestones** set up in previous reviews (either confirmation review or previous APR).
  - By the end of your **second year**, you must have:
    - the first chapter of your dissertation ready for submission to an academic journal;
    - a draft of the second chapter of your dissertation.
  - By the end of your **third year**, you must have:
    - the second chapter of your dissertation ready for submission to a journal;
    - a draft of your third chapter.
  - You should start thinking about both the job market and your thesis submission early in your fourth year.
- **Conference Attendance**
  - By the end of your second year, you should have presented at one domestic conference.
  - By the end of your third year, you should have presented at one international and/or domestic conference.
- Requirements by **Business School** and **GRS**
  - Attending necessary inductions, going through training etc.

## Additional Considerations

- If you are tutoring or lecturing (either at UNSW or other institutions), you need to report these activities during your APR.
- Depending on your circumstances, the panel members may advise on the desirability of modifying your teaching load.
