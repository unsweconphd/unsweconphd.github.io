## Timing of Confirmation Review

- The confirmation review must occur within the **twelve months** since the start of your PhD studies.
- If you start your Phd program in Term 1, 2023, it will be scheduled for **November** or **December 2023**.

## Procedure of the Confirmation Review

- If you are up for confirmation in December, notify the PGRC in **late October**.
- Consult with your supervisors to discuss your preferred **panel members**, and send the PGRC a list of names.
  - This part of the process is not mandatory but is highly recommended.
  - The panel members will provide you feedback throughout the stages of your dissertation.
  - You may also want to nominate a chair for the review panel.
- In consultation with your supervisors, the PGRC will appoint **three panel members**.
- Once the confirmation review panel is appointed, contact them to see **when** they are **available** for the review.
  - You must find a **date** and **time** when all panel members and your supervisors can attend.
  - Note the confirmation review can be held online.
- The SOE office will set up a venue for the review and handle the logistics.
  - But it is your responsibility to confirm date/time for the review and whether it will be in-person or online.
- You must fill in all the **paperwork** on [GRIS](https://gris.unsw.edu.au/login) and send relevant documents to the supervisor and panel members.
  - Fill in all the required forms on GRIS minimum **one month** prior to the review.
  - Include the milestones you and your supervisors agree upon for this confirmation.
- It is a **requirement** of the Business School that you submit the [PhD PLO assessment](https://unsw-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/z3485733_ad_unsw_edu_au/EZdCJAJfIYBCrnbtah0T6gQB1it2PHIJc9ZPkman-gaU5w?e=rxHeNd) [form](https://unsw-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/z3485733_ad_unsw_edu_au/EZdCJAJfIYBCrnbtah0T6gQB1it2PHIJc9ZPkman-gaU5w?e=rxHeNd) signed by your supervisor and upload to GRIS.
  - Alternatively, please upload the [signed form here](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=pM_2PxXn20i44Qhnufn7o7iTWtY-FCxHt53dD3ImtK9UNjZOSkw3SFlTNEdRMDJPRjRDU1E2MU5UVSQlQCN0PWcu).
- Send the presentation material to your panel members **at least a week** before the confirmation.

## Format of the Confirmation Review

- You will have **30 to 40 minutes** to present your own work:
  - Literature review.
  - Preliminary results and/or a preliminary draft of a paper, which will constitute the first chapter of your dissertation.
  - A thesis proposal; i.e., concrete ideas for the remaining chapters of your dissertation.
- The panel members will have **10 minutes** for questions.
- The panel will then ask you to leave the room and discuss among themselves and your supervisors.
- As soon as the panel reaches a decision, you'll be called back and receive their feedback.
  - The panel members will set new **milestones** for the next annual progress review.

## Minimum Requirement for the Confirmation Review

- **Coursework**
  - Direct Entry PhD students:
    - Passing all core courses with average grade of 85, each with grade 75 or above.
    - Being on the trajectory of passing all other courses with grade 75 or above.
  - Former MPDBS students:
    - Passing courses each with grade 75 or above.
- **PhD Seminar Series**
  - One lecture and one presentation.
  - Three referee-style reports.
- **Research**
  - You must have a good understanding of the literature you are researching.
    - This is usually accomplished with a literature review.
  - You need to present some preliminary results you have accomplished.
    - A research proposal with only a literature review is not acceptable.
  - A rough draft of a paper, which would constitute the first chapter of your dissertation.
  - Proposals for chapters two and three, with appropriate timeline.
- Requirements by **Business School** and GRS:
  - Attending necessary inductions, going through training etc.

## Unsatisfactory Confirmation Review

- If you don't meet the minimum requirements, the outcome of your confirmation review will be **marginal**.
- You will be given a maximum of **three additional months** to satisfactorily meet the requirements.

## Additional Considerations

- If you are tutoring or lecturing (either at UNSW or other institutions), you need to report these activities during your APR.
- Depending on your circumstances, the panel members may advise on the desirability of modifying your teaching load.
