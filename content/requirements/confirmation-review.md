## Timing of Confirmation Review

- The confirmation review takes place in your first year of the PhD. Full-time candidates hold it after 9 to 12 months of study and must be confirmed within 15 months.
- If you start your PhD program in Term 1, 2026, it will be scheduled for **November** or **December 2026**.

## Procedure of the Confirmation Review

- If you are up for confirmation in November or December, notify the Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC) in **late October**.
- Consult with your supervisors to discuss your preferred **panel members**, and send the PGRC a list of names.
  - This part of the process is not mandatory but is highly recommended.
  - The panel members will provide you feedback throughout the stages of your dissertation.
  - You may also want to nominate a chair for the review panel.
  - Panel members cannot be anyone who has been on your supervisory team, academics supervised by your supervisors, or your supervisors' active research collaborators.
- In consultation with your supervisors, the PGRC will appoint **three panel members**.
- Once the confirmation review panel is appointed, contact them to see **when** they are **available** for the review.
  - You must find a **date** and **time** when all panel members and your supervisors can attend.
  - Note the confirmation review can be held online.
- The School of Economics (SOE) office will set up a venue for the review and handle the logistics.
  - But it is your responsibility to confirm the date and time of the review and whether it will be in person or online.
- Complete your review form in [GRIS](https://gris.unsw.edu.au/login) (the Graduate Research Information System) at least **one month** before the review, and send relevant documents to your supervisors and panel members. Attach to the form:
  - your progress against your milestones, including evidence that you meet the minimum requirements below;
  - your presentation slides;
  - evidence that you have completed UNSW's research integrity and research data management training;
  - the milestones you and your supervisors agree upon for the next 12 months, and a timeline for completing your thesis;
  - the [PhD Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) assessment form](https://unsw-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/z3485733_ad_unsw_edu_au/EZdCJAJfIYBCrnbtah0T6gQB1it2PHIJc9ZPkman-gaU5w?e=rxHeNd), signed by your supervisor. This is a **requirement** of the Business School.
- If you miss the review or do not complete the GRIS form, you will receive a show cause request.
- Send the presentation material to your panel members **at least a week** before the confirmation.

## Format of the Confirmation Review

- You will have **30 to 40 minutes** to present your own work:
  - Literature review.
  - Preliminary results and/or a preliminary draft of a paper, which will constitute the first chapter of your dissertation.
  - A thesis proposal; i.e., concrete ideas for the remaining chapters of your dissertation.
- The panel members will have **10 minutes** for questions.
- The panel will then ask you to leave the room and discuss among themselves and your supervisors.
  - You may also meet the panel without your supervisors present.
- As soon as the panel reaches a decision, you'll be called back and receive their feedback.
  - The panel will review your **milestones** for your first annual progress review.

## Minimum Requirement for the Confirmation Review

- **Coursework**
  - Direct entry PhD students:
    - An average of at least 85 in the core courses, with at least 75 in each.
    - Being on track for an average of at least 75 in any other courses.
  - Former MPDBS students:
    - An average of at least 75 in your PhD courses.
- **PhD Seminar Series** (see [PhD Seminars Overview](/phd-seminars/phd-seminars-overview/))
  - Two presentations over the academic year.
  - Discussant duty.
  - At least 80% attendance.
- **Research**
  - You must have a good understanding of the literature you are researching.
    - This is usually accomplished with a literature review.
  - You need to present some preliminary results you have accomplished.
    - A research proposal with only a literature review is not acceptable.
  - A rough draft of a paper, which would constitute the first chapter of your dissertation.
  - Proposals for chapters two and three, with an appropriate timeline.
- Requirements of the **Business School** and the Graduate Research School (GRS):
  - Completing UNSW's research integrity and research data management training, and any required inductions.

## Unsatisfactory Confirmation Review

- If you don't meet the minimum requirements, the outcome of your confirmation review will be **Marginal** or **Unsatisfactory**.
- The panel will set conditions, and a second confirmation review will be held within **three months** (for full-time candidates).
- If you still don't meet the requirements at the second review, you will be asked to show cause why your enrolment should continue.

## Additional Considerations

- If you are tutoring or lecturing (either at UNSW or other institutions), you need to report these activities at your confirmation review.
- Depending on your circumstances, the panel members may advise on the desirability of modifying your teaching load.

For the full rules, see the UNSW [Higher Degree Research Policy](https://www.unsw.edu.au/governance/policy/policy-hub/hdrpolicy) and its [HDR Progression Instruction](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/pdfs/governance/policy/hub/hdrprogression.pdf).
