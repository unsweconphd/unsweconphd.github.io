These dates assume you started in Term 1. If you started at another time, count from your start date and check with the Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC).

## Every year

| When | What | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| Early January | Applications due for the PhD International Visiting Program | [Funding available](/funding-and-travel/funding-available/) |
| January–February | Send the PGRC your conference pre-approval email | [Request conference funding](/funding-and-travel/conference-funding/) |
| Mid-February | The PGRC announces the expected conference funding per student | [Funding available](/funding-and-travel/funding-available/) |
| Mid to late February | PhD International Visiting Program outcomes announced | [Funding available](/funding-and-travel/funding-available/) |
| Final third of the year | More funding may be released, depending on the School's remaining budget | [Funding available](/funding-and-travel/funding-available/) |

## MPDBS year

| When | What | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| End of Term 1 | The PGRC tells you which two academics you will work for as a research assistant | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| Term 2 | 50 hours of research assistant work: 25 hours for each academic | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| 30 September | Research assistant report due | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| End of October | Tell the PGRC who your supervisors are | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |

## PhD Year 1

| When | What | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| End of April | Agree milestones for your confirmation review with your supervisors | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| Late October | Tell the PGRC that you are due for confirmation | [Confirmation review](/coursework-and-reviews/confirmation-review/) |
| November or December | Confirmation review | [Confirmation review](/coursework-and-reviews/confirmation-review/) |

## PhD Years 2 and 3

| When | What | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| By the end of each year | Annual progress review, within 12 months of your previous review | [Annual progress reviews](/coursework-and-reviews/progress-reviews/) |

## PhD Year 4

| When | What | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| Middle of Year 4 | Job market paper ready to submit | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| At least two months before you submit | Lodge a Notification of Intention to Submit in GRIS | [Thesis submission](/thesis/submission/) |
| Around September | Submit your thesis. A PhD must be completed within 4 years of full-time study | [Thesis submission](/thesis/submission/) |
| October | Start assembling your job market package | [Candidature timeline](/getting-started/timeline/) |
| At least one week before your oral examination | You and your primary supervisor receive the examiners' reports and questions | [Before the oral](/thesis/oral-examination/#before-the-oral) |
| About eight weeks after your thesis is sent to your examiners | Oral examination, if your PhD started in Term 1 2023 or later | [At the oral](/thesis/oral-examination/#at-the-oral) |

## Before a review

| When | Confirmation review | Annual progress review |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|
| At least two months before | — | Discuss timing with your supervisors and the PGRC |
| At least one month before | Complete the review form in GRIS, with its attachments | Tell the PGRC the date, title and abstract; complete the review form in GRIS, with its attachments |
| At least two weeks before | — | Send your paper to the panel |
| At least one week before | Send your presentation material to the panel | — |
| At least two days before | — | Send your presentation material to your supervisors and the panel |

## Before a seminar presentation

| When | What |
|------|------|
| Two weeks before | Send your title and abstract to Sussan Su (<q.su@unsw.edu.au>) and to <econ.hdr@unsw.edu.au> |
| One week before | Send your draft paper and/or slides to your discussant |
| Two days before | Circulate your title, draft paper and slides to all PhD students and the PGRC |

GRIS is the Graduate Research Information System. For other terms, see the [glossary](/getting-started/contacts/#glossary).
