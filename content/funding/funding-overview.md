## School of Economics Funding

Research activities funded by the School of Economics (SOE) include the following:

- Conference participation.
  - Airfare, accommodation, and registration fee for one domestic or international conference.
  - Depending on the budget situation, the SOE may provide funding to cover additional conference expenses.
- Journal submission fee.
- Support for editing service.

The amount of support depends on the overall financial situation of the SOE and UNSW. The Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC) will announce early in the year the expected amount per student (around mid-February).

- If you are planning to go to a conference in Term 1, please talk to the PGRC so that funding can be made available at an appropriate time.

## Other Sources

- Experiments: BizLab fund up to AUD 3,000
- **Development and Research Training Grant (DRTG)**
  - Graduate Research School funding towards conference and training costs.
  - See the [UNSW HDR Hub](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/unswhdr) for details (UNSW login required).
- **PhD International Visiting Program**
  - Competitive grant to spend up to three months visiting an international institution.
  - Funding is up to AUD 12,000.
  - Applications are due early January.
  - Outcomes will be announced mid to late February.
  - A visit also needs your supervisors' support, an approved variation of candidature to work away from UNSW (apply in GRIS), and a Pre-Trip Approval.

## Tutoring/Lecturing

- The SOE offers tutoring and lecturing opportunities for Master of Pre-Doctoral Business Studies (MPDBS) and PhD students.
- Allocations are done depending on the teaching and tutoring requirements of the SOE.
- If you are interested in tutoring or lecturing, please discuss this first with your **supervisors** and the PGRC.
- Note that the School of Economics requires you to disclose tutoring and lecturing activities during the Confirmation and Annual Progress Reviews.

## Conference Funding Procedure

- Discuss with your **supervisor** which conference(s) you should participate in.
  - You should do this **early in the year**.
  - Each January–February, send the PGRC one pre-approval email covering all the conferences you plan to apply to (see the [sample email](/miscellaneous/conference-email/)).
  - The [EconBiz calendar of events](https://www.econbiz.de/Events/Results) lists forthcoming conferences in economics.
  - You can also consult the American Economic Association's [Meetings and Conferences](https://www.aeaweb.org/resources/conferences-meetings) page.
  - Funding will be subject to your **supervisor's approval**.
  - In general, the SOE will provide funding to attend **one conference** per year.
- Once your paper has been accepted to a conference:
  - Notify the PGRC and specify your funding request.
    - Be specific: make an estimate of airfare, accommodation and conference fees.
  - The PGRC will confirm with you the amount the SOE will provide.
  - Once you have this, lodge your Pre-Trip Approval and make the necessary bookings as soon as possible.
  - The UNSW travel approval process takes time, and you must follow the travel section of the UNSW [Finance Policy](https://www.unsw.edu.au/governance/policy/policy-hub/financepolicy).
    - Most importantly, every trip needs a Pre-Trip Approval, and **all bookings** go through FCM, UNSW's travel management company, via [MyTravel@UNSW](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/MyTravel).
  - Follow [this link](/funding/travel-approval/) for more information on the travel approval process.

## Additional Support

- Journal submission fees.
  - To receive support, you must obtain an endorsement from your supervisor.
  - Please include the name of the journal you plan to submit the paper to and the cost of submission.
- Editing services.
  - Requests are approved on a case-by-case basis.
- Dropbox licence.
  - UNSW provides OneDrive cloud storage for all its higher degree research students.
  - If you work with coauthors (including your supervisors) and you need to obtain a Dropbox licence, contact the PGRC.
  - Requests are approved on a case-by-case basis.
