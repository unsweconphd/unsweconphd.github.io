## School of Economics Funding

Research activities funded by the SOE include the following:

- Conference participation.
  - Airfare, accommodation, and registration fee for one domestic or international conference.
  - Depending on budget situation, the SOE may provide funding to cover additional conference expenses.
- Journal submission fee.
- Support for editing service.

The amount of support depends on the overall financial situation of the SOE and UNSW. The PGRC will announce early in the year the expected amount per student (around mid February).

- If you are targeting going to a conference in the first trimester, please talk to the PGRC so that funding at an appropriate time can be made available.

## Other Sources

- Experiments: Bizlab fund up to AUD 3,000
- **PhD International Visiting Program**
  - Competitive grant to spend up to three months visiting an international institution.
  - Funding is up to AUD 12,000.
  - Applications are due early January.
  - Outcomes will be announced mid to late February.

## Tutoring/Lecturing

- The SOE offers tutoring and lecturing opportunities for MPDBS and PhD students.
- Allocations are done depending on the teaching and tutoring requirements of the SOE.
- If you are interested in tutoring or lecturing, please discuss this first with your **supervisors** and the PGRC.
- Note that the School of Economics requires you to disclose tutoring and lecturing activities during the Confirmation and Annual Progress Reviews.

## Conference Funding Procedure

- Discuss with your **supervisor** which conference(s) you should participate.
  - You should do this **early in the year**.
  - [EconBiz](https://www.econbiz.de) collects a database on forthcoming conferences in economics.
  - You can also consult the American Economic Association [website](https://www.aeaweb.org).
  - Funding will be subject to your **supervisor's approval**.
  - In general, the SOE will provide funding to attend **one conference** per year.
- Once your paper has been accepted to a conference:
  - Notify the PGRC and specify your funding request.
    - Be specific: make an estimate on airfare, accommodation and conference fees.
  - The PGRC will confirm with you the amount the SOE will provide.
  - Once you have this, please make the necessary booking as soon as possible.
  - UNSW travel approval process takes time. But yo must follow UNSW rules ([procedure](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/pdfs/governance/policy/2022-01-policies/travelprocedure.pdf) and [policy](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/pdfs/governance/policy/2022-01-policies/travelpolicy.pdf)).
    - Most importantly, **all booking** must be done through [MyTravel](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/MyTravel).
  - Follow [this link](/funding/travel-approval/) for more information on the travel approval process.

## Additional Support

- Journal submission fees.
  - To receive support, you must obtain an endorsement from your supervisor.
  - Please include the name of the journal you plan to submit the paper to and the cost of submission.
- Editing services.
  - Requests are approved on a case-by-case basis.
- Dropbox license.
  - UNSW provides One-Drive cloud storing space for all its higher degree research students.
  - If you work with coauthors (including your supervisors) and you need to obtain a Dropbox license, contact the PGRC.
  - Requests are approved on a case-by-case basis.
