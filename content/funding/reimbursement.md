Use the [ER1 form](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/finance-hub/Shared%20Documents/Forms/AllItems.aspx?id=%2fsites%2ffinance-hub%2fShared+Documents%2fstudent_expense_reimbursement_form_er1.pdf&parent=%2fsites%2ffinance-hub%2fShared+Documents&isSPOFile=1&OR=Teams-HL&CT=1727408109525) (UNSW login) for all claims, such as Airbnb stays and conference registration fees.

1. Enter the relevant items on the ER1 form.
2. Get the form signed by the Postgraduate Research Coordinator (PGRC).
3. Send the following to Sussan Su (<q.su@unsw.edu.au>):
   - the signed ER1 form;
   - relevant emails from the PGRC;
   - bank transactions showing your payments.

The claim is lodged in [myexpenses](https://www.fin.unsw.edu.au/myexpenses) (UNSW login) with these details:

- **Code**:
  - Fund: OP001
  - Dept: FBS121
  - Project ID: PS74434
  - Project Description: SDDRESE Res Stdnt Sppt – ECON
- **Approver**: Hock Thye Chan
- **Attach the following documents**
  - tax invoice
  - bank statement
  - relevant email(s) from the PGRC
