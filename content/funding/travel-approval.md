## Pre-Trip Approval Process

### What to do?

1. Go to [MyTravel](https://unsw.sharepoint.com/sites/MyTravel) website.
2. Read through the information and login to **MyPortal** (at the end of "prepare to book" tab).
3. Choose "Pre Trip Approval".
4. Choose "Travel Request".

You will be asked to provide information regarding your trip.

- Traveller's information.
- General information about your trip.
  - Choose "Conference" and upload the following documents.
    - Email from the PGRC stating the approved amount of budget.
    - Conference acceptance letter.
    - Other relevant documents.
- Travel plan.
- Travel Budget
  - Use the following project codes:
  - Fund: OP001
  - Dept: FBS121
  - Project ID: PS74434
  - Project Description: SDDRESE Res Stdnt Sppt – ECON

### Approvals and notifications

- **Primary approver:** SOE manager (Hock Thye Chan)
  - DO NOT use your supervisor's name.
- **Budget owner:** SOE manager (Hock Thye Chan)

### Notes

- The SOE will only support the **pre-approved amount**.
- If your expense goes above the pre-approved amount, then you'll be later asked to pay back.
  - You may want to check with your supervisors if they can provide additional support.
- You are required to book through **MyTravel team** except under special circumstances.
  - Accommodation in Airbnb or travel by bus could count.
  - Ask the SOE office before making any purchase outside the standard travel procedure.
- It is **good practice** to search for trips yourself before going to MyTravel.
  - Once you find a convenient, inexpensive alternative, ask MyTravel team to book it for you.
