You can consult the [website](https://www.business.unsw.edu.au/students/scholarships/postgraduate-research/phd-placement-scheme) for further information

## Overview

- There will be two funding rounds each year, in April and September, and it is anticipated that a maximum of fifteen scholarships, of a value of up to $3,000 each, will be awarded each year.
- The application will be evaluated by a committee.
- You have to expend the budget before the UNSW financial closing date, i.e., no carry-over
- Two rounds in a year, April and September.

## Eligibility

- Applicants must be enrolled in a PhD program in the UNSW Business School and have completed at least five semesters full-time (or equivalent) at the closing time of submission of applications.
- Students will only be awarded the scholarship once during candidature.
- Students must have applied for PRSS before applying for this scheme.
- Students must have participated in the Business School 3MT Heat or Student Research Fair.

## Application procedure

- The UNSW Business School Research Office will email a call for applications to all enrolled PhD well in advance of the application closing date for each founding round.
- Applications should be submitted to the School Postgraduate Research Coordinator.
- A committee consisting of the School Postgraduate Research Coordinator, Director of Postgraduate Research, and Associate Dean (Research) or his/her nominee will assess each application taking research accomplishment (excellence) and potential for placement into account. The school of the nominee will not be taken into account in ranking decisions, except in terms of benchmarking each nominee’s research performance by discipline.

## Allowable scholarship expenditure

- The scholarship funds can be used to assist with travel costs associated with securing a prestigious placement post-PhD.
- Relevant activities may include attendance at a significant conference or travel related to visiting potential employers. Requests for any other activities can be considered but must be fully justified.
- Activities directly relating to applicant’s research thesis in progress cannot be funded.
- Not allowable activities include travel for data collection, hiring research assistants or purchasing computing equipment.
- Each scholarship awarded will be placed into a research account accessible by the awardee’s school administration, which must be expended by the UNSW financial closing dates for that calendar year.
- Any funds not either expended directly or reimbursed prior to those end-of-year closing dates will be forfeited by the awardee – funds cannot be carried over to the next calendar year.
